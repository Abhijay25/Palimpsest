## Searchable heatmap iteration

`static/heatmap.js` now drives `/explore`; the old skyline-only JS remains as provenance and is no longer loaded. A compact all-question skyline sits above a virtualized question matrix. The real cohort is unchanged: 324 all-time questions, 313 represented by the latest observations in the initial yearly columns, 128 in the latest checkpoint. Do not confuse these scopes. Rows are unbounded and scrollable; only visible rows are rendered. Year/Month/Week/Day buttons, period navigation, double-click zoom, category/pair filters, observed category-change filtering and keyboard arrows operate on stored data. Borderless adjacent colored cells provide visual continuity; explicit unknown gaps and the sampling notice must remain. A missing row finding is not agreement or resolution.

`heatmap.py` caches a bulk selected-evidence catalogue. `GET /explore/api/matrix` and `/cell` perform no model work. The explicit CSRF-protected `POST /explore/api/search` combines BM25 over source-derived labels and selected representative excerpts with MariaDB cosine similarity to their existing multilingual vectors. Local E5 query embeddings use `embedding_model(local_files_only=True)` and a bounded query cache. No model download, Decisions call, translation generation or new historical assessment is part of search. Semantic retrieval contributes at most 40 candidates above the heuristic similarity threshold; keyword matches are uncapped. This search limit is not a discovery/topic cap. If the local model is unavailable the UI explicitly reports keyword fallback.

“Most controversial” is an explained ordinal sort: possible contradiction, framing, then context, scoped to displayed observations and actual pair classifications. It does not use passage counts or claim calibrated controversy. Pair-specific category metadata and exact occurrence IDs survive the evidence preview and historical article handoff. Search, filters, selected cell, observation, scroll and date window are kept in the return URL. Initial label text uses source sections/question types, without new generated summaries.

## Current semantic zoom and corpus pipeline

The active `/explore` template loads `static/constellation.js` and `static/constellation.css`. The earlier skyline/heatmap files are preserved experiments. One full viewport canvas shows a month's question dots; zooming out shows twelve monthly blobs, then yearly blobs. Category colors stay amber/orange/red. Blob totals are period UNION cardinalities, with each question assigned its strongest actual selected-pair category within that period. Do not sum observations, language pairs, passages or child-month totals. Month dots and their parent blob must agree. Unknown and outside-anchor periods remain visibly distinct from observed zero. The date selector lists the latest eligible checkpoint per UTC day; the month union still includes all stored observations.

`zoom_read.py` serves period aggregates, paginated questions and dynamic corpus article lookup through `/explore/api/periods`, `/constellation` and `/articles`. The page fetches every question page in bounded parallel batches; it does not cap topics. Search is collapsed and dual-scope: dataset articles or current questions. Keyword results arrive first; local hybrid search refines after a short idle interval. Stored source/title indexes are reused. Query-scoped GET search may compute a local embedding but never calls a paid provider, downloads models, prepares history or generates prose. Source context always remains selected-language evidence.

Question naming is an explicit background step in `question_titles.py`: narrow structured Responses generation proposes a specific neutral question from bounded representative evidence; cached Decisions grounding checks and source-ID/scope validation accept or retain provisional labels. Accepted titles are presentation metadata, not grouping identity. `question_search.py` persists keyword documents and uses existing multilingual passage vectors. The only newly allowed generative provider stage is `question_titles`; other free-text generation remains disabled. Database locks cap title workers at two and total provider concurrency at six across processes. Failed reservations remain retained; no blind retry or ledger reset.

`article_pipeline.py` removes Winter War-only preparation scope through article/edition anchors, article-scoped run/cache/group identities, snapshot/history jobs and a durable MariaDB queue. The local dataset currently has 7,256 eligible multilingual article IDs (EN/FI/SV); that number is corpus eligibility, not completed comparisons. New history preparation assesses monthly as-of checkpoints plus bounded recent daily revision checkpoints, reusing unchanged revision tuples and passage embeddings. Existing legacy runs remain readable. First validate Winter War, then a ten-article pilot; enqueueing the remaining corpus is explicit and separate. Do not claim the entire corpus has been assessed merely because article lookup lists it.
