# Language Gap Explorer

Audience: Wikipedia editors investigating differences between language editions. The primary task is reading a source passage and its nearest counterpart, then deciding whether to investigate. Success is a local real-data demo with traceable excerpts, direction reversal and transparent uncertainty. No automatic edits or claims of factual error. Local Finnish/Swedish-to-English machine translations help readers review evidence. The full supplied corpus is searchable; paid comparison remains an explicitly queued pilot.

The update watchlist lets an editor confirm a relationship between passages in two or three editions. A subsequent sandbox/imported revision to either side triggers a persistent review notice with the exact diff, counterpart and translation. This is a change alert, not a new contradiction judgment. Diff detection and translation need no paid model calls; Decisions is an optional follow-up. Reviews, notes and baseline revisions persist in MariaDB. The seeded date-change example is explicitly a sandbox demonstration; live Wikimedia subscriptions and automatic propagation are outside this MVP.

Tornedalians showcase: Article / Compare languages / Update tracker share navigation. Snapshot comparisons remain pinned to August 2025. Historical replay uses actual edits ending at those snapshots, event-time counterpart retrieval and persistent reviews. Sandbox examples stay outside the showcase.

Winter War experiment: a fourth Semantic timeline tab follows a bounded 50-edit history in English, Finnish, Swedish and Russian. Its primary task is selecting an edit and reading exact source changes, with locally translated evidence. Decisions labels factual, framing and coverage changes; it does not establish truth, editor sentiment, motives or political alignment. Historical counterpart retrieval is explicit and preserves the current edition on the left. Russian is a separately pinned supplement, not an extension of the supplied three-language corpus. This experiment does not add live monitoring or publish edits.
