A row tracks a specific question or claim across editions. Each segment is a period with a distinct relationship between the selected passages. Hover for a preview; select a segment to compare its evidence.

Darker means more divergence.

Light segments indicate complementary context or a retrieval-limited evidence gap; medium segments indicate different framing; dark segments suggest incompatible claims. Gray segments need more context. No retrieved match is not proof of an omission from the whole article. These categories guide review. They are not a measure of truth or a calibrated severity score.
