Reading this timeline

Facts, framing and information changes are assessed on exact, numbered before/after excerpts using batched Decisions predicates. A semantic-change score of at least 0.80 and a category score of at least 0.80 are required for those labels. Uncertain and unassessed evidence stays labeled. Scores describe the assessment, not factual correctness.

Normalized prose is compared on both sides. Tables (including infoboxes), reference lists and exact paragraph moves are excluded. This is a prose timeline, so table and citation edits can be missed. Historical HTML is rendered today; templates/transclusions may reflect newer versions. Hidden or unavailable revisions are shown as gaps. Long passages are split and marked as incomplete context. Titles are generated by code from section names, labels and changed values.

Compare editions at this time explicitly retrieves the nearest multilingual passages from your edition as it existed at or before the edit time, capped at its dataset anchor. MariaDB ranks the three closest candidates from that revision. Similarity does not establish agreement or contradiction. Your edition stays on the left; the changed edition stays on the right.

No editor intent or national bias is inferred. Local English machine translations can help with review but may introduce errors. Nothing here publishes a Wikipedia edit.
