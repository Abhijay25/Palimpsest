SCOPE AND TIE-BREAK RULES (apply before choosing a verdict):

Unchanged text carried over from `OLD_TEXT` is out of scope.
- G1 vs G2: Use `FLAG_G1_FACT` only when the edit changes WHAT is claimed — a truth value, number, date, entity, or quantifier scope ("some" -> "all") — so that `NEW_TEXT` contradicts the source. Likewise framing changes (G2) are judged on wording, regardless of silence.
  * Use `EXPAND_CONTEXT` only when the window itself is broken (a pronoun whose referent is missing, a truncated sentence or table) or when it visibly points to the missing fact.
